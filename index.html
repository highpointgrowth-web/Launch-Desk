How it works

Attach a phone number and link the client's booking calendar. Availability syncs automatically — the agent never double-books or offers dead slots.

Flip the agent on. Every call shows up in your dashboard with a transcript, outcome, and booking status — proof you can send straight to your client.

Questions

Straight answers.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. If you can fill out a form and connect a calendar, you can put an agent live. LaunchDesk handles the voice infrastructure, telephony, and calendar sync under the hood.

What niches does LaunchDesk support?

Right now: plumbing, HVAC, and electrical. These are the highest-intent home-services verticals — phones ring constantly and missed calls cost real money. More templates coming.

How does the AI agent actually book appointments?

The agent connects to your client's calendar, checks real-time availability, and books directly into open slots during the call — no follow-up needed, no double-booking possible.

Can I white-label the agents for my clients?

Yes, on the Agency plan. Your clients hear their own business name, not LaunchDesk.

What phone infrastructure does LaunchDesk use?

LaunchDesk is built on enterprise-grade voice AI infrastructure. You get a dedicated number per agent with low latency, natural-sounding voices, and full call logging.
