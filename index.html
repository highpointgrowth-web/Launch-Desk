Questions

Straight answers.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. If you can fill out a form and connect a calendar, you can put an agent live. LaunchDesk handles all the voice infrastructure, telephony, and calendar sync.

What niches does LaunchDesk support?

Any niche where businesses receive inbound calls — plumbing, HVAC, electrical, dental, legal, roofing, and more. You pick your industry when building an agent.

How does the agent actually book appointments?

It connects to your client's calendar, checks real-time availability, and books directly into open slots during the call. No follow-up, no double-booking.

Can I white-label the agents for my clients?

Yes — on the Agency plan. Your clients hear their own business name, not LaunchDesk.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Cancel from your account settings and your plan ends at the billing period.
